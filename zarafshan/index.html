On the Curry Mile, since day one.

Wilmslow Road's stretch of curry houses and grills has been a Manchester institution for decades. Zarafshan sits right on it, serving Afghan and Turkish fusion cooking — halal throughout, kitchen open into the early hours for the after-dark crowd the Mile is famous for.
