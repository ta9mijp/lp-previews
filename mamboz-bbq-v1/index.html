Mamboz BBQ is a Tanzanian grill kiosk at 170 Gascoigne Road, Barking — the sign on the counter reads "Best Tanzanian Delicious Foods." Skewers, whole fish and chicken are cooked over open coals to order, alongside biriani, pilau and Zanzibar-style street food. Rated 4.6 from 37 reviews on Google.
