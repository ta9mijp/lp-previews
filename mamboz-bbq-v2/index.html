The Sign

A red sign on
Gascoigne Road.

Mamboz BBQ trades from a red-signed kiosk at 170 Gascoigne Road, Barking. The banner above the counter reads "Best Tanzanian Delicious Foods" — skewers, whole fish and chicken are grilled over open coals, alongside biriani, pilau and Zanzibar-style street food.

The stall is rated 4.6 from 37 reviews on Google, drawing a steady stream of regulars from across Barking for its grill and its Zanzibar Mix.
